Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 includes a number of updates and improvements over the developer's past games, and that includes new weather systems. Rockstar's vice president of art for lighting and rendering at Rockstar, Owen Shepherd, told Game Informer that the studio has "re-engineered" the studio's weather system to "make everything more more localized and persistent." He said the goal was to make the weather in GTA 6's setting of Leonida--a stand-in for Florida--feel "dynamically solid and achieve a sense of scale" that the studio has never done before. GTA 6 launches on November 19. What that means in practice is that GTA 6 will have "fully dynamic cloudscapes that roll across the map" to provide a "broad spectrum of weather" in the game. Florida is known for its heat and humidity, along with its localized and fast-moving rain systems. Look one direction and you may see bright sunshine. Look the other and you could see a downpour. GTA 6 will replicate this with localized rain showers, along with rainbows that appear. Additionally, places where it recently rained in the game will remain wet, with puddles also popping up. GTA 6 also has a new wind system that includes "offshore breezes after sunset, convectional updrafts, and occlusion from buildings,” Shepherd said. “Hair and debris will swirl around on rooftops; gusts of wind will blow ripples over the water surface and bend the grass in swamps.” GTA 6 will also include tropical storms and hurricanes, along with thunder and lightning. Shepherd did not, however, get into how GTA 6's new weather systems could affect gameplay. Florida is known to experience devastating hurricanes, but it remains to be seen if, for example, a hurricane could knock down structures or trees. GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, priced at $80 for its standard edition and $100 for the Ultimate Edition. Rockstar is also selling a $400 collector's edition that includes a bunch of physical goodies, but not the game.

That's strong on its own, but there is currently a bugged interaction with Hellfire Torch that is allowing players to equip two Unique charms in addition to Hellfire Torch instead of one, granting even higher levels of power.  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2L55dgNzpQ8 The bug seems to stem from Hellfire Torch being used first alongside the Seal of the Golden Epiphany, which allows players to equip up to three Unique charms. However, players are later swapping out Golden Epiphany for the Mythic Seal of the Diamond Mind, a seal that reduces the number of charms needed for set bonuses, but are still able to benefit from having additional Unique charms equipped. That, in turn, allows players to gain advantages from a full five-piece set bonus, Hellfire Torch, and two Unique charms all at once.  Now, Diablo 4 design director Dan Tanguay in a forum post said Blizzard is aware of the bug and said Hellfire Torch' s current behavior is "beyond what was originally intended." "We know many players have spent significant time earning the Hellfire Torch and building characters around it," Tanguay said. "Because of that, we want to be thoughtful about making adjustments mid-season that could disrupt those investments." Tanguay continued: "We've also seen the positive reception to the Hellfire Torch itself from many players. The item has created a lot of excitement this season, and we've taken note of the feedback from those who have enjoyed earning it and incorporating it into their builds. While we don't have anything to announce today, it is something we're considering for the future, which makes it even more important that we fully understand and address this interaction the right way." The post states Blizzard will continue to investigate the issue so that any future changes will be "implemented correctly and behave as intended."  Players in Season 15 just might be stronger than ever before. Alongside the bugged Hellfire Torch interaction, players can also use this trick to get around the game's crafted Mythic item restriction and fully deck themselves out in Mythic gear. It's just the last development in what has been one of Diablo 4's wildest, and most surprising, seasons yet. Season 15, Hell's Legacy, introduced all kinds of new content based on previous Diablo games, including boss fights against Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto ripped straight from earlier entries in the franchise. It's also filled to the brim with secrets upon secrets, including a showdown with the cow god Moophisto and even the return of the secret pony level, Whimsyshire, from Diablo 3.
