"It really started by looking at who our players are," Zitterman said. "Who are Diablo players? Why are they playing Diablo? What do they want out of a Diablo game? How do we do this?' Looking at past examples, looking at what's working well in Diablo 4, where there are opportunities for improvement, and we kind of build our ambition for the game at the conceptual phase through those avenues of information." https://youtu.be/Y8HaSFXR7tU "And as we started to understand and kind of solidify what that ambition was, it became very clear to us pretty early that in order to achieve what we wanted to achieve with this game, we needed to make some more deeply structural changes to the way the game works, changes that are just frankly hard to do in an expansion. We want to tell a story that's on par with--and hopefully exceeds--the story we told with Lilith and Mephisto." "And to do that in an expansion just didn't feel right. We needed to give him his own game." Player feedback also plays a vital role in the development of Diablo 5, and Zitterman said that by announcing the game this early, Blizzard could make important changes well ahead of the game's eventual launch. "If we didn't announce now and we kind of held back until we were much further along in development, at that point, it's much harder to take that feedback and use it to make those really important changes and refinements to the game throughout development," Zitterman explained. If Blizzard is adding 1,000 more quests to WoW with the update, how many does that bring the game up to in total? It's a tricky question to answer, Greenfield said, in part because some of WoW's original quests are "tracking quests," but if you're counting "actual playable quests," the game has around 3,000 to 3,500. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IEMrosrAVH0 So with 1,000+ more added of actual playable quests, that's a significant increase. He went on to say some of the original WoW's quests were dark and grim, and Blizzard is embracing that philosophy with a lot of the new quests. "Even Zephyr Isle, it's in the sky, it's nice and bright, but it's the story once you get into it, it's not it's not all, you know, sunshine and rainbows up there.
