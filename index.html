More Than Anything, Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil Movie Understands How It Feels To Play A Resident Evil GameNEW
In 2006, I eagerly attended the first showing of Christophe Gans’ Silent Hill. I loved Silent Hill 2, but was ready to mock the film, as video game adaptations to that point had been und...
In 2006, I eagerly attended the first showing of Christophe Gans’ Silent Hill. I loved Silent Hill 2, but was ready to mock the film, as video game adaptations to that point had been underwhelming, to say the least. In one scene, protagonist Rose checked her map, and I leaned over to my friend and said, “Now she just needs to check the map every few seconds to make it really feel like Silent Hill.” To my surprise, Rose proceeded to check the map on every block as she made it to her destination. The movie leading up to that scene and the rest following it is generally forgettable, but that moment made me pause and realize that Gans had in fact played Silent Hill, and was probably even a fan.Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil made me recall that moment from Silent Hill. A moment that expressed the feeling of playing Silent Hill more than just being a direct adaptation of the property. As promised by Cregger in multiple interviews promoting the film, Resident Evil contains very few overt references to the larger Resident Evil franchise. It primarily takes place in Raccoon City, the Umbrella Corporation is acknowledged, there is a typewriter, and protagonist Bryan uses Heavy Field brand shotgun shells. That’s about it. But the whole film expertly expresses the feeling of playing a Resident Evil game. The intense, terrifying highs, the cathartic moments of catching your breath, the frantic pace, the moments of failure as a player, the reward of simply figuring things out, and even building your skill over the course of the game finally paying off in the end.The movie follows Bryan on the worst night of his life. He is a normal guy (who doesn’t know how to use a gun) trying to make some money on a delivery job while managing his feelings during an emotionally tense event currently affecting his girlfriend and himself. And then he gets stuck in a Resident Evil game – and there is no Leon to whisk him away on a jet ski.From that point forward, Bryan reacts to everything not as a Resident Evil protagonist, but as a player of the games. He stares wide-eyed at unbelievable biological horrors; cannot stop comically swearing as he sprints full speed away from danger; screams at embarrassing pitches when monsters leap from the shadows; makes myriad mistakes; searches rooms for anything that could help him; knows he shouldn’t open that scary door even though he has to; is constantly wasting ammo; and during one of my favorite moments, accidentally gets rid of an item he absolutely needed. The whole debacle (which lasts a sublime 90 minutes) is intense, scary, fun, and most surprisingly, often hilarious.Director Zach Creggor and Austin Abrams, who plays Bryan, nail a very important element of Resident Evil that I believe no other Resident Evil movie has been able to figure out: you can’t take this too seriously. Resident Evil is absurd. The Resident Evil characters are the Leslie Nielsons of this Naked Gun horror universe, and we as the players are the ones that get to scream and laugh and have a good time. Bryan is our stand-in in Creggor’s adaptation, and I love it for that. I don’t need all video game adaptations to be like this. There is undeniable joy in seeing specific elements of a property you love getting the Hollywood investment and being projected on a big screen. I have no idea if it will be good or bad, but I would be lying if I said I wasn’t excited for the Zelda movie next year. But I love that the emotional experience of playing a Resident Evil game has been so expertly translated into a film here. It officially unseats my previous favorite video game film adaptation, which was, of course, Josh Ruben’s 2021 take on everyone’s favorite Ubisoft game, Werewolves Within.

How To Use The Aniimo Nurturing FeatureNEW

Aniimo is a complex creature-catching game, and one of the big features of the world revolves around nurturing. This allows you to raise the Prismana Flow in the area, increase your chances of encountering special Aniimo, and more. You want to be utilizing the nurture feature early on in your playthrough, so you can progress faster and encounter more Aniimo types. In this guide, we'll explain what nurturing means and how you can quickly level up this feature. Nurturing system and blooms explained Bloom icon As you explore Idyll, you'll encounter various nurturing blooms in the world. These blooms will be marked as a dark blue tulip-shaped icon on your map. Interacting with a bloom can allow you to trigger a "wild surge" of Aniimo that are native to that area of the map. With a wild surge triggered, you will have a designated circle around you and tons of Aniimo will spawn. You are tasked with capturing all the Aniimo that appear within a specific time limit. If successful, the bloom releases Prismana Energy and increases the Prismatic Energy State of that specific map area. Raising the Prismatic Energy State will increase your chance of a Prismana Aniimo appearing. This means you may encounter one early in your game, but you also need to complete several nurture attempts before reaching the required energy level. How to Start a nurture Nurture bloom Interact with a nurture bloom, and choose the nurture option near the bottom-left of your screen. You can also check the Prismana Aniimo associated with an area through the map. The silhouettes shown for each bloom on the map can also help you identify which Prismana Aniimo can appear within the area, which is helpful if you're trying to collect a specific one. Starting a Nurture typically requires Vain Essence Stones, but you also receive three free nurture charges each day. Once you use one, another charge will slowly recharge each day. It's best to use your free charges each day so you don't waste any opportunities to level without paying a cost.   Adding shards to nurture Adding shards as tribute Shards are special items that can be added as a tribute before nurturing a bloom. They come in different elemental types, and the type of shard you use affects which Aniimo can appear during the wild surge event. You'll see the option in the bottom-right corner of your screen to add/remove a tribute. Using one of these shards gives you more control over which Aniimo you encounter during the surge. A Grass Shard will attract the grass-type Aniimo in the area, while a Dark Shard will attract the dark-type creatures. You can obtain shards in a few ways: Scavenge them from vases, stumps, and other environmental items Buy them from the shop vendor at the main outpost Possibly earned through rewards Collect Prismatic Energy after wild surges  Capturing Aniimos will slowly work to increase the Prismatic Energy State of the area, but completing wild surges from blooms is how you want to work to quickly raise the levels. Interacting with a bloom after a successful wild surge will release a boost of Prismatic Energy to the area. This increase will not only give a chance for a Prismana Aniimo to spawn, but it can also give a chance to encounter evolved Aniimo or the "shiny" sparkling variants. Nurturing menu Completion of wild surges and leveling the Prismatic Energy State is a slow grind, but you'll notice all bloom regions start out as "empty," tasking you with fully raising the levels over time. Prismatic Energy level colors are: White (empty) Green Blue Purple  Orange As you reach higher levels, it costs more to nurture, but your chances of getting those rare spawns (Prismana, evolved, sparkling) increase as well. How To Unlock All Vampirism Abilities In The Blood Of Dawnwalker – Blood Phial Locations GuideNEW

Do you want to unlock all Vampirism abilities in The Blood of Dawnwalker? As someone who turns into a creature of the night, you have several ways to reach your full capabilities. However, you must feast not only on hapless foes, but also on the vrakhiri lords themselves. Our guide has all the details that you need, but be forewarned that there will be spoilers, too. Vampirism abilities - Unlocking via corruption, quests, blood phials, and bosses The Blood of Dawnwalker Vampirism abilities are quite unlike the ones you see in the Witchcraft and Swordmastery trees. The Witchcraft and Swordmastery perks primarily require the use of skill manuals, which you need to read to unlock and subsequently upgrade said options. Vampiric abilities, meanwhile, are acquired through corruption, blood phials, and bosses, as well as certain notable quests. Vampirism abilities from corruption levels The vast majority of vampiric abilities that you unlock in the game come from corruption levels. Basically, you need to drink blood from various sources, such as humans, animals, and kobolds. You can also increase corruption levels a bit by drinking vials and consumables that restore your health. Abilities that can be unlocked or upgraded include Voracious Bite, which is required for vampiric healing, and Shapeshift, which turns you into a wolf to speed up exploration. Several perks like Dancing Claws, Endless Ferocity, Blood Theft, and more are also dependent on your corruption levels. The only exceptions to this mechanic are Piercing Shriek, Shadowstorm, Mesmerize, Blood Surge, and Scarlet Shield, which are unlocked via other sources. In fact, if you highlight these skills, you’re simply told that you need to “drink vrakhiri blood.” Chowing down on hapless foes increases your vampiric corruption level. Vampirism abilities from vampire boyar bosses Some unlockable Vampirism abilities in The Blood of Dawnwalker come from slain vrakhiri boyars--i.e. the major bosses/lieutenants of Brencis. You can refer to the following guides if you need help taking them out: Ambrus - The Gilded Gauntlet Bakir - The Lunar Game Xanthe - The Cycle of Love To obtain these abilities, you must defeat a vrakhiri boss and drink their blood when presented with a dialogue choice. You will then get transported to a hellish realm called Anachora, and you have the option to invite their soul into Coen’s being. These are the abilities that you acquire from bosses: Scarlet Shield - Unlocked by drinking Ambrus’ blood. This grants a chance to regenerate your HP after a perfect block/block. Shadowstorm - Unlocked by drinking Bakir’s blood. This slows down time around you. Blood Surge - Unlocked by drinking Xanthe’s blood. This causes a target to explode, while bosses take tremendous damage instead. Defeating major vrakhiri bosses like Xanthe gives you the option to drink their blood to gain their powers. Vampirism abilities from legendary blood phials That said, there might be instances where, instead of drinking a vrakhiri boss’ blood, you decide to kill them outright. Alternatively, you might take them up on a deal that they offer in exchange for their survival. If that’s the case, you can still unlock vampiric skills by collecting and drinking that vrakhiri’s blood phial. A map showing the locations of the legendary blood phials. The legendary blood phials in The Blood of Dawnwalker come from (a) certain locations you stumble upon while free-roaming and (b) vampire court activities that you can track via the Court menu: 1x Phial of Brencis’ Blood - Found inside the cave of the Beast of Balaur. It’s also where you can pick up the Gladius legendary weapon. 1x Phial of Xanthe’s Blood - Found inside the treasury room near the Svartrau City cathedral complex. You can learn more about this in our article regarding Father Florin’s quest arc. 1x Phial of Bakir’s Blood - Looted from the Bloodletter elite that appears during one of Ambrus’ vampire court activities. 1x Phial of Brencis’ Blood - Collect the blood from the reliquary during Xanthe’s Recruiters: Taste of Power. 1x Phial of Ambrus’ Blood - Collect the blood from the reliquary during Xanthe’s Recruiters: Just a Bit More. 1x Phial of Bakir’s Blood - Collect the blood from the reliquary during Xanthe’s Recruiters: Come Home. You can also convince the elite blood guard to surrender so you don’t have to fight him. Note 1: Drinking Brencis’ Blood Phial unlocks the Piercing Shriek ability.
