How To Get A Parachute In GTA 6 (And Best Places To Use It)NEW
When you choose to add items to your character's loadout in Grand Theft Auto 6, you likely prioritize weapons and body armor over anything else. After all, these are what protect you from enemies and ...
When you choose to add items to your character's loadout in Grand Theft Auto 6, you likely prioritize weapons and body armor over anything else. After all, these are what protect you from enemies and can make the difference between living and dying during a firefight. While you certainly want to make sure you always have weapons and armor in GTA 6, you'll also want to make sure you have a parachute equipped. A parachute is a highly underrated item to have in your loadout, as it comes in handy if you steal a plane or another aircraft and need to bail. The parachute is also extremely fun to use, especially if you're trying to complete Base Jump challenges. Below, we'll go over where you can find a parachute, as well as some of the best locations to make use of it in GTA 6. Where to find a Parachute in GTA 6 There are a couple of places where you can acquire a parachute in Leonida. The easiest spot to add a parachute to your loadout is at any Ammu-Nation store across the map. These stores are marked by a pink gun icon on the map, and they are commonly found in every region. At an Ammu-Nation, you'll find a section for parachutes, often near the back wall with the body armor. Each parachute sells for $250, and you can immediately equip it to your loadout. You can view whether you have a parachute equipped by holding L1/LB and going to the "Items" tab of your loadout. Here, go to the bottom-left section, and you'll see the icon for the parachute (if it's equipped). You can choose whether to hide the parachute or make it visible on your back. Either way, as long as the parachute is in your loadout, you'll be able to use it when the time comes. If you don't want to pay for a parachute in GTA 6, you can grab one for free by visiting any of the 25 Base Jump locations in Leonida. These locations are mostly located on the roofs of the tallest skyscrapers across Vice-Dale County. You'll often be able to enter and ascend any building that has a visible "H" helipad on the roof. On the roof's helipad, you'll sometimes see a parachute surrounded by lights and energy drink cans. This indicates the location is a Base Jump, and you can equip the parachute to complete the challenge. However, it will consume your parachute, so if you just want the item, then leave the roof without actually doing the Base Jump. Best locations to use a Parachute in GTA 6 Now that you have your parachute in GTA 6, you'll need some specific locations to make use of it. While you can keep it in your loadout and save it for a rainy day, where's the fun in that? We found a few set spots in Leonida that give you the best views and opportunity to use a parachute, and here's where they are: Maven Residences Building (downtown Vice City) First up is one of the tallest buildings in all of Leonida. It's called the Maven Residences building, and you can find it directly across the street, southwest of Panos Plaza in the east part of downtown Vice City. You can freely enter the building, take the elevator to the roof, and then go to the helipad to jump off. This offers one of the best overall views in all of GTA 6. Mount Kalaga/Mainfest Canyon This next spot is a given. Mount Kalaga in southern Lummox County is the highest point in Leonida, and it provides the perfect opportunity to use a parachute. However, finding an exact spot can be difficult, as the mountain's peak is fairly flat and doesn't have a perfect jumping-off point. For that, you might want to head over to Mainfest Canyon in northeast Kelly County. This doesn't have nearly the height of Mount Kalaga, but there are sharper peaks that make for a better jumping experience. Crimson Palms Condominiums (north Washington Beach) Our final dedicated parachute spot is on top of the Crimson Palms building in the northern part of Washington Beach (east of Vice City). This building is more like a monument than anything, and it looks to be the tallest skyscraper in the entire game. The views from the top of this building are incredible, as you can look out at the ocean or towards downtown Vice City when you jump off the top. Firaxis, the studio behind the Civilization series, has laid off around 60 employees, representing a third of its staff. The report comes from Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier.In a statement provided to Bloomberg by a spokesperson from 2K, a subsidiary of Firaxis' owner, Take-Two, “Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength—designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years. As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.”In addition to the 60 affected staffers, Firaxis studio head Heather Hazen is also departing. Bloomberg also reports, via anonymous Firaxis employees speaking to the outlet, that the studio is working on an unannounced XCOM game that has had a challenging development, with the team recently missing an important development milestone. This project, if it releases, will be the first Firaxis-developed XCOM game since XCOM: Chimera Squad in 2020.Firaxis’ most recent release is Civilization VII, which launched in February 2025 to favorable reviews but initially "slow" sales, according to Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick last August.  The game currently has a Mixed reception on Steam after Firaxis spent the last year updating the game to address player complaints. Before Civilization VII, Firaxis shipped Marvel's Midnight Suns in 2022, another critically well-received game that failed to meet sales expectations. [Source: Bloomberg]

The Blood of Dawnwalker is one of the year's biggest, most ambitious games, and the developers over at Rebel Wolves have done a good job cleaning up bugs and performance issues in the weeks since launch. Today, that trend continued; the new 1.2 update is out now on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC, and it adds New Game Plus. The new mode is uniquely ideal for a game like Dawnwalker. The narrative is very player-driven, whether it has to do with your literal dialogue options, your ability to manage your vampiric hunger, or the order you complete quests, and all of these elements can cause your experience to vary wildly. It makes sense, then, that players would want to jump back in to try things again and see what might happen if the game is played differently, and New Game Plus allows them to do that while keeping combat fresh.According to the update notes on the Dawnwalker website, "NG+ unlocks after you complete the game’s Epilogue and lets you experience the story again while keeping your progress – mainly your character level, skills, and inventory." You don't gain access to these skills until you complete the prologue, however, which marks the adventure's proper start.The new mode will also rebalance the world's level scaling. In the base game, certain areas are much harder than others, but in New Game Plus, all enemies will rise in power to match your level, meaning you can explore the world in whatever order you want. On that note, the level cap has also been raised to 100, and protagonist Coen can collect new, more powerful versions of weapons marked with an "NG+" label.That's basically everything in this update. Rebel Wolves also added some slight tweaks to combat in the base game and adjusted some geometry in the open world, but the main focus here was New Game Plus. This is in contrast to the 1.1 update, which added a much wider suite of bug fixes and made the game run more smoothly.
