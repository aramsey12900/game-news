Four-player online cooperative play joins the original fun of two-player local. Unfortunately, I encountered several instances of framerate chugging and stuttering when the screen got especially crowded, even if the game otherwise ran smoothly throughout my playthroughs. I enjoy the new location aboard a unique Covenant ship, especially the expansive concluding event, though the scope of these three missions feels less impactful overall compared to many Halo adventures over the years.  Master Chief and Cortana’s journey across the ringworld of Halo was an integral experience in my gaming life, and I’m certainly not the only one. Campaign Evolved is a superb update and rebuild of what made the original great, but it stops short of a complete restructuring of that landmark game. Even constrained by some of the features that defined the 2001 classic, this new version nails the roiling current of chaos that made it so fun all those years ago.   Score: 8.5 About Game Informer's review system
