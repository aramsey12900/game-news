Also in the interview, Cohen revealed that he's pouring $500 million of his own money into his bid for eBay.

At the very least, remakes offer a great way to play antiquated or less accessible experiences on modern hardware. The remake boom has been in full swing in recent years to the point that it’s starting to get tough to keep track of all the projects in the works.
