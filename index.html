From the moment you set foot on your new farm, you'll be presented with a bag of turnips and a bag of tulips. These are two out of four of the main plant types in Fields Of Mistria--vegetables and flowers, alongside tree fruits and foragables. Your farm will need a bit of TLC before you can plant anything, but the sooner you get those seeds down, the closer you are to making your first real money in Mistria.  The process of planting is simple. Find a clear spot in your farm, use the hoe to carve a square of tilled land, plant your seeds, then water them. If you keep up your watering schedule, each morning will bring a new stage of growth, all the way up to harvest time. Rainy days are a farmer's best friend, though, as crops will water themselves.  Different crops thrive in different seasons. Crops will only grow in their allocated season, though, and the moment Spring rolls into Summer, or Fall into Winter, your crops will wither and die, even if they were just about to bear fruit. Each season lasts 28 days, so plan carefully--especially with regrowable crops, or ones with expensive seed bags. Remember you can store your unplanted seeds indefinitely, they won't go bad, so if you've missed planting season this year, keep them for the next one.
