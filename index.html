Golf is one of the most popular sports in the real state of Florida, so it's no surprise that Grand Theft Auto 6 offers plenty of ways to play golf in the fictional state of Leonida. GTA 6 offers two types of golf: mini-golf and traditional 18-hole golf, both of which are fully accessible from the start of the game. Leonida also features multiple courses for whichever version of golf you decide to play. There are some slightly different rules for mini-golf versus regular golf, but the mechanics for either of them will make sense after a couple of holes. Below, you can see all of the golf course locations in GTA 6 and how to play like a pro. The course locations are fairly spread out in Leonida, and the mini-golf courses all feature different themes, making the holes look and feel extremely varied. You can see the names and locations for all five of the golf courses in GTA 6 below: Traditional Golf Courses Leaf Links Golf Club Located just north of Washington Beach, this location returns from the original Vice City. In GTA 6, Leaf Links is its own island, centered around a full 18-hole golf course with sprawling ocean views and some of the wealthiest houses in all of Leonida. Though Mar-a-Lago doesn't seem to be in the game, this course probably comes closest to capturing its vibe. Check the bathrooms for classified documents. Mount Hailes Club Located in north Waning Sands, Mount Hailes Club is a well-maintained course that feels like a standard country club golf experience. This location is easier to break into than Leaf Links, but you'll still need to watch for security near the front doors. Mini-Golf Courses Dino Golf Challenge Dino Golf is an abandoned mini-golf course in Frogswater, Kelly County, that you can play for free, but it's the least impressive golf course in GTA 6. Frontier Adventure Golf Frontier Adventure Golf is located in south Waning Sands right near an outlet mall and racetrack, making it a perfect way to enjoy the suburban life in GTA 6. The course is themed around the Old West. King Gator Adventure Golf King Gator Adventure Golf is a course south of Buckskin Swamp and is themed around Leonida's most iconic creature: the alligator. How to play golf in GTA 6 If you've played or watched any golf in real life, you'll immediately understand what the rules are for both mini-golf and traditional golf in GTA 6. Starting with mini-golf, at any of the three courses, all you need to do is walk up to the front gate attendant and pay for a club and ball. You can choose to play the full 18 holes, the front nine holes, or the back nine holes. Once you pick and grab your club, head to the first hole or the ninth hole, depending on which play option you picked. The game will also mention that you can invite the other protagonist to join you, which helps grow the love between Jason and Lucia. From here, you can set your ball in one of three positions at the starting spot for any hole. Choose your position, then hold L2/LT to aim the ball. You can either use your left stick or R2/RT to shoot the ball by holding down the stick or button to build up power. Before shooting, you're also allowed to change your stance using the right stick if you prefer shooting left or right-handed. Once you shoot your ball, go to where it stops and repeat the process until the ball goes into the hole. Also, remember to pick up your ball from the hole before moving on to the next hole. Traditional golf is much more time-consuming and involved than mini-golf in GTA 6. However, since both courses are in private country clubs, you will have to avoid security if you want to play. The best route to take is to find an empty golf cart and use it to drive the full 18-hole course. If you want a club and golf balls, there's usually a free bag to take containing both of those items just lying around on the courses. This is the only way we found to actually play the courses. With your cart, club, and golf balls, just play as you normally would. Go to the starting tee, line your ball up, aim, and shoot. Playing the full 18 holes can take a fairly long time, but playing golf does raise your focus in GTA 6, so it's a worthwhile piece of side content. You can sit back and let Jason or Lucia take the wheel.
