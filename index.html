A good game is a good game, even if it doesn't make your PC cooling system emit gusts of air hot enough to melt the skin on your hands. For GameSpot's first-ever Cheap Week, we're looking at experiences that can entertain you for hours on end and don't require cutting-edge hardware. If you've been rocking the same mid-range laptop since 2016 or you recently got an entry-level PC before prices went through the roof, these games should run just fine on your system. What's our baseline? For this list, we looked at games that can comfortably be played with zero or few graphical concessions. We're talking games that run fine at 1080p--preferably at 60fps--are compatible with a wide range of configurations, and are contemporary. Ideally, if you have 4GB of RAM, an old-school GPU like the Nvidia 1060, or an onboard chipset that you'd find in most laptops, these games should work just fine on them.
