Table of contents How to sell Droid Blueprints in Fortnite Droid Tycoon Where to sell Blueprints in Fortnite Droid Tycoon How to sell Droid Blueprints in Fortnite Droid Tycoon Screenshot by Destructoid Blueprints can be sold to the Jawas at the Sell Blueprint stand close to the main cantina area in the center of the Droid Tycoon map. There are two Jawas working at this booth, and bringing your unwanted Droid Blueprints to them is the only way to sell them off for Credits. Once you have a Droid Blueprint you wish to get rid of, approach this booth and throw the Blueprint at it to sell it off. The amount of Credits and XP you're given in return will then be displayed for the transaction. You always get fewer credits than what you paid or what you can get for selling the fully crafted version of the Droid Blueprint, but when you don't have space or need to check off a quest, the selling option comes in handy for turning a quick profit. Sometimes, the selling process can be a bit finicky, which means it may not work as intended when you throw a Blueprint at the stall. If this happens, swiftly pick it up and try again. Be sure to act fast here, as another player may try to swoop in and take off with your Blueprint if it's left unattended on the ground for too long. Where to sell Blueprints in Fortnite Droid Tycoon The exact location where your base spawns in Droid Tycoon varies each time you load up this mode. Regardless of where you pop up, though, make your way over to the building in the center of the map, right next to the Sandcrawler, where you can purchase Droids. At the building in the middle of the map, circle around to the front side, which is the side facing the Sandcrawler. To the left of the circular event stage attached to this building, and to the right of the neon sign on the front of the cantina, you'll spot a small booth with the words "Sell Blueprint" on the front. This booth is the spot where you can sell your Droid Blueprints. Screenshot and remix by Destructoid There's plenty more to uncover and get done in Fortnite's special Star Wars event.
