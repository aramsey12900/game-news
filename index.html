The southern islands have a beach that’s full of rotting sharks. WAiNK Curiosity #11: Wetman Bay Carcass Speaking of rotting stuff, there’s yet another alien creature thingy carcass on the island to the north. WAiNK Curiosity #12: NoFlusher Homestead This GTA 6 WAiNK Curiosity is due west of the Kingfisher swamp base and, truth be told, it might make you feel queasy.
