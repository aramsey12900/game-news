Zero Parades feels like an extension of Elysium's ideas at times, allowing CASCADE to overexert her faculties for a higher chance of success, accruing anxiety, fatigue and delirium all the while, threatening to leech away hard-earned skill points when any meter goes beyond its breaking point.
